Privacy

Nexternel runs on your own equipment at home. We do not collect or receive your data.

Anything you connect, such as a Google Nest thermostat, talks directly between your Nexternel and that company, and its sign-in details stay on your own Nexternel. That company's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

The one exception is paid add-ons: to unlock one, your Nexternel contacts our licence service with your licence key.

Contact: support@nexternel.com
